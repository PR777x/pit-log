What's in the pit

01

Your shows

The setlist of your life

Every gig you've hit, laid out like a stack of torn ticket stubs. Lineup, venue, tour, who you went with. Flip to the calendar to see your whole year of noise at a glance.

02

The night

Rate the carnage

Skulls for how good it was. Pit intensity from polite sway to wall of death. Where you stood, best band of the night, the moment you'll be telling people about for years.

03

The Vault

Posters, photos, merch haul

Keep the tour poster, the blurry phone shots and the shirt you overpaid for. The Vault sorts it all by band or venue, so the memories are one tap away.

04

Crew

Bring the crew

Add your friends and see their shows next to yours. Tag them on the nights they were there, find the gigs you hit together, and link logs with Crew Family so a show added once lands in both.

05

Discover

Find the next one

Fresh tour dates for PA, NJ, DE and NY every morning. One card per tour, flagged when it stops at a venue you've been to, with tickets and one-tap add. Hide anything you'll never go to.

06

Bucket list

The bands you still need

Keep a list of the bands you haven't caught yet. When they announce a date nearby, it shows up in Discover and your Sunday recap, so you're never the one who found out too late.

07

Quick add

Log it before the ringing stops

Type a band or tour and Pit Log finds the show, from your crew's logs or the announced listings, and fills in the rest: lineup, venue, date and tour.

08

Stats

Receipts for your obsession

Shows attended, bands and venues, miles driven, what you spent on tickets and merch, your average rating, plus badges for the barricade regulars.

Before you jump in

Does it cost anything?

No. Pit Log is free.

Who can see my shows?

You choose. Each detail (ticket cost, photos, ratings, notes and more) can be Private, Crew Family, Crew or Public, and your crew only sees what you allow.

Is it only for metal?

Log anything with a stage. The tour listings lean heavy (metal, rock, punk and alternative) because that's who built it.

Will it flood my inbox?

No. At most one evening email on days something actually happened, and a Sunday recap. You can turn either off.

Can I get it on my home screen?

Yes. Open thepitlog.com in your phone's browser and choose "Add to Home screen." It opens like an app.
