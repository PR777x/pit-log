Privacy

Last updated November 14, 2026

Pit Log is a concert diary. This page explains, in plain English, what it keeps and who can see it.

What we keep

  • Your account: your email address (to sign in and send the emails you choose), your name, username, profile photo and bio.
  • Your log: the shows you add and everything you put on them: bands, venues, dates, ratings, notes, setlists, who you went with, ticket and merch spend, posters, photos and merch pictures.
  • Your lists: bucket list bands, tours you mark Interested or hide, and your settings.
  • Basic error reports: if the app crashes we save the error message, browser type and app version so we can fix it. Nothing from your log is in them.

Who can see it

  • Your email address is never shown to anyone.
  • Each kind of detail has its own setting under Profile → Settings → Privacy: Private, Crew Family, Crew or Public. New accounts start with spending, notes and where you stood set to Crew, and the rest Public.
  • "Crew" means people you've accepted. "Public" means anyone signed in to Pit Log can see it on your profile and in band search.
  • You can block anyone. Blocked people can't find you, add you or see your profile.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your data or show ads. Pit Log runs on a few services that handle data on our behalf: Google Firebase (sign-in, database, photo storage), Resend (sending emails), Ticketmaster (tour listings shown in Discover) and setlist.fm (setlists, only when you look one up or import). Tapping a Tickets or Spotify link takes you to that site, which has its own privacy policy.

Emails

We email you to confirm your address, to welcome you, and for the notifications you leave on (the evening digest, the Sunday recap and show reminders). Every email has links to turn them off, and you can change them under Settings → Notifications.

Your choices

  • Download: Settings → Your data → Download my data gives you a copy of every show.
  • Delete: Settings → Your data → Delete my account permanently removes your account, shows, photos and crew links. Shows you shared with Crew Family stay in their log as their own copy.
  • Or email support@thepitlog.com and we'll do either for you.

Kids

Pit Log isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly let them sign up.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app or by email.
